About NFSU & NFAT 2025

National Forensic Sciences University (NFSU) is an Institute of National Importance known for its advanced programmes in forensic sciences, cyber security, law, criminology, and investigative sciences.

NFAT 2025 (National Forensic Admission Test) is the common entrance test for admission to multiple NFSU programmes across various campuses. Candidates must:

  • Carefully read the official admission brochure and eligibility criteria.
  • Fill the online application form with correct details and required documents.
  • Download the NFAT admit card from the official portal before the exam.
  • Follow all instructions mentioned in the admit card and exam guidelines.
Note: Ineligible candidates can be disqualified at any stage of the admission process. Students are advised to rely only on official NFSU notifications for final dates, merit lists and counselling schedules.
