🔔 NFSU Admission Notice 2025

Complete details on registration, NFAT exam, eligibility, and admission schedule.

📌 Admission Overview

National Forensic Sciences University (NFSU) has opened admissions for the academic year 2025–26. Registration is being conducted online through the official portal until May 5, 2025.

Important: NFAT 2025 will be conducted on June 7 & 8, 2025 for all eligible applicants.

📅 Key Dates for Admission 2025

  • Registration Deadline: May 5, 2025
  • NFAT Entrance Exam: June 7 & 8, 2025
  • Merit List Release: June 23, 2025
  • Counselling Round 1: June 30, 2025

🎓 Courses Offered

  • MSc Forensic Science
  • MSc Cyber Security & Digital Forensics
  • Criminology & Forensic Psychology
  • Professional Diploma in Crime Scene Management
  • BSc, PG Diploma & Certificate Courses

Eligibility Note

Candidates must verify eligibility carefully. Ineligible applicants may be disqualified at any stage of the admission process.
