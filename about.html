About NFSU Info

Empowering students with updates, counselling guidance, and trusted resources.

🎯 Who We Are

NFSU Info is a student-led digital platform designed to keep aspirants and current students of the National Forensic Sciences University (NFSU) informed and guided. We share official updates, important notices, and personalized counselling support for admissions, results, and internships.

📢 Mission

To make authentic NFSU-related information easily accessible and help every student make confident career decisions.

💡 Vision

To build India’s most trusted forensic education support platform — connecting students, mentors, and opportunities.

🤝 Our Work

We provide counselling guidance, study materials, updates, and admission assistance with complete transparency.

👥 Meet the Team

Team NFSU Info

Dedicated group of NFSU students and alumni helping aspirants through mentorship and authentic updates.
